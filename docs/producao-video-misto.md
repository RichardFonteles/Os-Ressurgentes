# Produção do vídeo misto

Filme ilustrativo de 30 segundos, 16:9, 1280×720, 24 fps. Sem locução ou música. Imagem sintética de quatro adultos, animada com aproximação suave; abertura e encerramento usam as imagens da página original. Textos explicam a proposta e o preço do piloto. Não representa participantes reais ou depoimentos.

A imagem foi gerada pelo recurso integrado imagegen. O filme foi montado localmente; a tentativa de geração de cenas no Higgsfield não criou trabalhos porque a conta exige plano Basic ou superior.

## Prompt final da imagem

Use case: ads-marketing. Create one photorealistic editorial cinematic landscape 16:9 image for a personal development landing page film called Os Ressurgentes. Exactly four fictional Brazilian adults aged 35-50, two women and two men with diverse skin tones, seated around a simple wooden table in a quiet room adjacent to a lush green garden. Natural respectful listening, one woman thoughtfully writing in an unbranded notebook, others attentive, no staged smiles, no looking into camera. No branded game board, only plain cards and notebooks. Camera wide medium shot including all four adults, composed scene, generous dark negative space along the bottom third for later typographic overlay, keep faces in upper half. Moss green, charcoal, warm ivory palette with subtle warm gold daylight from window. Beautiful film photography, natural skin texture, believable hands, everyday clothes, shallow depth of field but all faces legible. Atmosphere human, sober, hopeful, calm agency rather than miraculous transformation. No logos, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no product UI, no claims or testimonials. Landscape high quality.
